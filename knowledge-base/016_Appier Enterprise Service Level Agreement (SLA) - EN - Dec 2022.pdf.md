# 016 Appier Enterprise Service Level Agreement (SLA) - EN - Dec 2022.pdf

Appier Enterprise Service Level Agreement 
(Effective from 1 June 2019 – Updated Dec 2024) 
1. Definitions 
(a) Covered Service denotes the SaaS (Software as a service) services provided by 
AIQUA, AIXON, BotBonnie, AIRIS and Retail Media Network(RMN). This includes the core functionalities provided by these products, as well as the data injection reliability and availability of the underlying data platform. 
(b) Monthly minimum uptime percentage means the percentage of time within one calendar month that the services are available (excluding the maintenance time). The percentage can be found on our product status page. 
(c) Availability means the covered service is in operable status and can be accessed through various accesses (e.g. Web UI and APIs) as applicable to the system services. 
(d) Maintenance time is the planned time in which the system is scheduled to undergo maintenance activity, including, but not limited to, infrastructure upgrades, data migration, and new service deployment. 
2. Service Availability 
For each Appier enterprise product, Appier ensures a minimum monthly uptime percentage over 99%. 
For planned maintenance activity, the team will announce the maintenance period on the System Status Page (https://status.appier.com) at least 2 days in advance. Customers can check the status page on a regular basis to see if there is a planned maintenance activity. For unplanned maintenance activity due to urgent system updates, Appier will update the status page 1 hour in advance. Customers can subscribe to the Status Page so that any updates on any maintenance activity will be delivered via email/SMS/RSS feed. 
The customer acknowledges that it will not be able to access services during the scheduled maintenance window on those occasions when maintenance is actually scheduled or during unscheduled maintenance. Appier shall endeavor to provide advance notice to Customers of unscheduled maintenance that affects Customers. Appier shall indicate to Customers the reasons for and duration of any unscheduled maintenance. 
3. System Status Monitoring 
Appier provides, and Customer has access to, the Health and Status Dashboard available at https://status.appier.com. This dashboard uses a "green", "yellow", and "red" system. Green is
used to indicate the system is functioning as desired. Red is used to convey that an area of the system is being significantly impacted. Yellow is used to convey that a degradation of Services is occurring, but the issue has not been identified as causing a Service outage. Yellow is also used to indicate that a previously red status has been corrected and is being monitored for continued stability. In addition, system access latency is provided on the status page for reference. 
4. Service Support 
This section documents the support processes and standards used by Appier Customer Success teams to enable effective and consistent use of our Enterprise products. 
Hours of Support 
The Appier Customer Success organization provides remote product support and operates from 9 AM to 7 PM Monday through Friday (excluding normal business holidays) in each respective business markets Appier has customers in and will serve as the primary point of contact for reporting any Appier issue. The Customer Success organization provides first line technical analysis, problem solving and diagnostics. After hours support when required, is available at an additional cost. 
To ensure timely response of critical system issues, each enterprise product will have dedicated on-call engineers monitoring system availability 24x7. When any critical system issues are identified, on-call engineers will work with an appointed incident manager to update the system status on https://status.appier.com, as well as resolve system issues based on incident handling SOPs.

Customer Notification 
Appier communicates with customers via email, voice/video calls, or with in-person meetings (where applicable). 
Customer Escalation 
The Appier Customer Success organization is the single point of contact for all incidents to be reported to Appier. Please report any ticket or service issues to your designated Appier Customer Success Manager as the first line of support. 
If a ticket or a service issue needs to be escalated, please reach out to your designated Appier Customer Success Manager as the first line of support. They will direct you to the appropriate Regional Customer Success Lead for your region and product line (AIQUA, AIXON, AIXPERT, AIRIS, RMN, BotBonnie).
5. Root Cause Analysis 
For incident(P0) issues, Appier will perform internal postmortems for critical incidents within 48 hours of an incident being detected. The Root Cause Analysis report will be available upon request by the customer within 7 days after resolution of the Incident. 
6. Exclusions 
The Service Commitment does not apply to any Service unavailability due to performance issues such as those: 
(a) caused by factors outside of Appier’s reasonable control, including any Force Majeure event or Internet access or related problems. Force Majeure means a cause beyond such Party’s reasonable control, including but not limited to the weather, unavailability of utilities or communications services (including access to the Internet), civil disturbances, acts of civil or military authorities, (b) arising from scheduled downtime for system or network maintenance. Scheduled maintenance will be announced 2-days in advance, (c) Feature provided as “POC” and “Beta” level. Feature maturity will be clearly documented in our Product Resource Center, (d) arising from third-party platforms, such as impact from Meta’s and/or LINE’s associated API uptime status, resulting in Appier’s service linkages to these social media platforms being affected. 
7. Updates 
As our business evolves, we may change our Service Level Agreement. We will proactively notify Customers for the update of this SLA document.
