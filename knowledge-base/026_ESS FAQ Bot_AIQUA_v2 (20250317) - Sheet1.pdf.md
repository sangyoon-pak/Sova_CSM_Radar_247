What’s the strategy and how do you choose for recommendation? Recommendation models are AI models that train on data (i.e. the product ID parameter in user events and the information in your product data feed) to generate product recommendation results that are most likely to be relevant to your target audience. There are five categories of recommendation models: 1) User-based: based on individual user preferences and behaviors. 2) Product-based: based on the designated product to recommend products that are purchased together with or share similar attributes with the designated product. 3) Popularity: generate results from products that are considered popular among your site and app users 4) Advacned: Advanced recommendation models aren't just based on a single dimension, such as product attributes or user preference; instead, these models use multiple dimensions, are fully customized by Appier Professional Service, or are AI-selected. 5) Build your own: These models allow you to select the parameters and events used to generate recommendation results. 
Also, we offer Autopilot mode in which users can easily optimize the recommendation without choosing models on they own. AIQUA will automatically allocate the traffic to the best-performing models to optimize the result.
