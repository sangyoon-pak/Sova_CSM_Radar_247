# 012 Appier AIRIS  AI-Powered Customer Data Platform Overview

Appier AIRIS: AI-Powered Customer Data Platform Overview
What is AIRIS and what are its core functionalities?
AIRIS is Appier's next-generation AI-powered Customer Data Platform (CDP) designed to unify and activate customer data for digital marketers. Its core functionalities include real-time data collection from various online and offline sources, including websites, apps, CRM, POS, and ERP systems. AIRIS cleanses, structures, and enriches this data to create a single, comprehensive view of each customer. A key feature is its AI-powered capabilities, which provide predictive customer insights such as conversion and price sensitivity scoring, and automate the creation of predictive models. AIRIS also facilitates audience segmentation based on behaviors, demographics, and predictive attributes, enabling personalized and timely marketing engagement across multiple channels.
How does AIRIS ensure data quality and prevent wasted marketing efforts?
AIRIS incorporates a "Data Quality Booster" that standardizes, cleanses, and prepares incoming data for campaigns. This high-quality data enables more precise audience suppression, allowing marketers to avoid targeting users who have already converted or are unlikely to engage. By eliminating this waste, brands can improve their media efficiency and ensure that their advertising spend is more effective. Furthermore, AIRIS enhances customer understanding by combining clean data with AI-generated keywords, uncovering nuanced intent signals for better message tailoring.
How does AIRIS handle the challenge of identifying and tracking individual customers across different devices and platforms?
AIRIS addresses the complexity of customer identification with its multi-ID system. This system manages a hierarchy of identifiers, such as database IDs, email addresses, and cookies, in a dedicated sub-system. The goal is to accurately associate a user's actions across various touchpoints and devices (e.g., website visits from different browsers, app usage, email interactions). When a user interacts anonymously (e.g., via a cookie) and later identifies themselves (e.g., by signing in with an email), AIRIS can link these disparate identifiers to a single customer profile, providing a holistic view of their interactions.
What kind of predictive insights does AIRIS offer to marketers?
AIRIS provides several AI-driven predictive insights to empower marketers. This includes Conversion Scoring, which forecasts the likelihood of a customer converting, and Price Sensitivity Scoring, which predicts a customer's sensitivity to pricing. These dual analytics help in anticipating customer needs and tailoring campaigns for optimal performance and personalization. Additionally, AIRIS utilizes AutoML to build predictive models for various marketing outcomes, such as purchase likelihood and churn risk, and allows for tagging customers with predictive user attributes in real time.
How can businesses integrate their existing data sources with AIRIS?
AIRIS offers a "Data Loader" that seamlessly integrates with various data sources, including CRMs, databases, and data warehouses. This tool allows data teams to create detailed mapping tables and synchronize data in real time. For track imports (event data) and identify imports (attribute data), AIRIS requires specific information such as unique identifiers and timestamps. For data sources behind private subnets or firewalls, users need to add AIRIS's server IP addresses to their allowlist. Furthermore, the CSV Loader feature allows for importing data directly from CSV files and mapping those fields to corresponding AIRIS fields.
What reporting and analysis capabilities does AIRIS provide for understanding customer behavior?
AIRIS offers a range of reporting and analysis tools. Trends Reports allow users to visualize patterns in their data over time, analyzing combinations of user segments and dimensions. Retention Reports help understand if users continue to take significant actions over time, indicating engagement and loyalty. Cohort analysis tracks the behavior of a group of users who share a common characteristic over time. Additionally, AIRIS provides AI-powered visual reports that users can generate and customize without coding. The platform also supports segmentation based on various criteria, enabling focused analysis of specific user groups.
How does AIRIS facilitate marketing activation and integration with other tools?
AIRIS is designed for seamless marketing integration and activation. It allows users to create triggers based on customer events and segments, which can then be used to activate audiences in external platforms like Google Ads for retargeting campaigns. AIRIS also integrates with marketing automation tools, ad platforms, and CRM systems, enabling real-time audience synchronization and automated customer journeys. For instance, it can integrate with AIQUA (Appier's Personalization Cloud) and BotBonnie (Appier's chatbot platform) to deliver personalized engagement across web, app, email, SMS, and social messaging channels.
What are some key differentiators of AIRIS compared to other CDP vendors?
AIRIS distinguishes itself through several key aspects. It offers a seamless user experience from data unification and segmentation to marketing activation, reducing the need for data duplication and ensuring consistency across platforms, unlike some pure CDPs. Compared to emerging MA+CDP solutions, AIRIS emphasizes its superior data unification capabilities, leading to more accurate customer profiles. Standalone differentiators include real-time data collection, AI-powered visual reports that require no coding, and seamless integration with marketing tools for automated workflows. Additionally, AIRIS provides flexible customization for features like RFM analysis to align with specific business needs.
