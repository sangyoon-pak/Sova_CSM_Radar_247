Key Characteristi cs 
Large-scale, data-rich enterprises with complex OMO environments. High revenue/market cap, large staff, complex legacy tech stacks (e.g., SAP, BigQuery), massive customer databases (millions of members), and dedicated data teams. 
Lookalikes: Eslite, PX Mart 
Mid-market, digitally-native, or high-growth companies aiming for rapid market expansion or IPO. Agile teams, often founder/CEO-led, focused on 3-5 year growth strategies, and actively building their data foundation. 
Lookalikes: Mr. Living, ZIGExN, iStyle. 
Traditional industries and those in transition, often primarily based on offline stores, face pressure to prove the value of digital marketing and integrate it with offline sales. They have started to initiate the business model transformation, but find it difficult to learn the marTech solutions. 
Lookalikes: ASO, Wellcare, Hope Music.

Core Pain Points 
- Fragmented customer view (online vs. offline). - Slow data retrieval and analysis (>40 min reports) - Cumbersome segmentation in legacy systems. - Inability to activate massive datasets for personalization. 
- Untrackable purchase cycles. - Inefficient operations with current tools (e.g., separate DB and MA). - High learning curve for marketing platforms. - Need for sophisticated experimentation to find new growth momentum/strategy 
- Inability to attribute offline sales to online marketing. - Low website revenue from existing digital tools. - High-touch, manual service for common inquiries. - The team lacks capacity/expertise to leverage data effectively.
