Category Question Answer 
General question What is the Appier AI Agent? 
The Appier AI Agent is an advanced marketing auto-pilot designed to transform your strategy by unifying data insights, campaign automation, and creative generation. Its power comes from three core values: 
Industrial Expertise: The Agent is pre-trained on a vast Industrial Knowledge Base, giving you the strategic insights of hundreds of industry experts right out of the box. 
Future Prediction: It goes beyond historical analysis by using powerful AI to predict future customer behavior, allowing you to identify high-potential segments that would otherwise be missed. 
Autonomous Evolving: The Agent continuously learns from your unique data and campaign results, autonomously improving 24/7 to become a true, self-evolving expert for your brand. 
By combining these capabilities, the Agent eliminates data fragmentation, optimizes the customer experience, and delivers a massive efficiency uplift—all while achieving high ROI and easy deployment. 
General question What business challenges can it solve? 
It resolves data fragmentation to optimize the overall customer experience. 
It provides a significant Efficiency Uplift by using AI to set up campaigns/journeys and generate creative content (text, images). 
It enables Predictive Segmentation to go beyond rule-based audiences and find high-potential users that are often missed. 
General question What’s the difference between Appier Agent and general-purpose LLMs like Gemini or ChatGPT? 
The key advantages of Appier Agent are: 1. Performance powered by your own marketing data + predictive AI  Unlike general LLMs, Appier Agent is connected to your historical campaign performance, user engagement behavior, and Appier’s proprietary predictive AI models. This enables it to generate highly effective audience segments and campaign content optimised for your actual business goals. 
2. Built on best practices from 150+ leading brands  Appier has supported over 1,000 brands across industries and distilled successful playbooks from top performers. Appier Agent is trained with these real-world best practices, giving you a head start by building on what has already proven to work. 
3. Seamless execution with native integration to MA tools  All content, segments, and campaign logic generated by the agent can be directly synced into AQ settings. You only need to review and confirm — then the campaign can go live instantly, eliminating repetitive manual setup. 
General question What are the prerequisites for using Appier AI? 
Appier AI is built on top of Appier’s core products: Segment Agent is powered by AR (Appier’s CDP) — the industry-leading customer data platform. Campaign Agent is powered by AQ (Appier’s MA platform) — the industry-leading marketing automation system.
General question Will the AI learn from user interactions? (For the Insight/Audience/Campaign Agent) 
Although the current version does not yet support AI memory, it is already included in our Q4 product roadmap. We will introduce Agent Memory, enabling the agent to learn your preferences over time — such as your preferred KPIs, channels, and tactics (for example, “don’t send campaigns on Mondays”). 
With memory, the agent will continuously adapt to your style and operating rules, becoming a truly personalized assistant that understands how you work and proactively supports you with the best recommendations. 
Pricing Can Agents be purchased as standalone products? 
No, the Agents are not sold as standalone products. Each Agent requires a foundational Appier solution to function, as this provides the necessary unified data source. For example, the Audience Agent requires AIRIS, and the Campaign Agent requires AIQUA. This integration is key to their power.

Technology & Architecture What is the foundation and architecture of the Audience Agent and Campaign Agent? 
Appier Agents are built on top of Appier’s core data and marketing infrastructure — AR (CDP) and AQ (Marketing Automation Platform). 
They leverage a three-layer architecture: 1. Customer & Campaign Data Layer (AR + AQ Integration) - Unified customer profiles, engagement signals, transaction data - Real-time behavioral data and predictive scores - Ensures every AI suggestion is grounded in actual business data, not generic assumptions 
2. Domain Knowledge & Best Practice Layer - Trained using 150+ successful marketing playbooks from leading brands - Refined with knowledge from Appier’s domain experts and professional service teams - Encodes marketing logic such as funnel strategies, segmentation heuristics, timing strategies, and content frameworks 
3. AI Reasoning & Execution Layer - Generates actionable segments and campaign content - Natively executes by syncing to AQ settings, enabling instant activation without manual setup

ROI Agent What is MMM (Media Mix Modeling)? 
MMM is a statistical analysis method that evaluates how different marketing channels contribute to business outcomes (such as sales or conversions) based on historical data. It helps marketers understand which channels drive ROI and how to optimize budget allocation.
ROI Agent What’s the advanced of MMM? The difference between different incrementality test 
Unlike other incrementality tests that require testing or holdout groups — which can cause opportunity loss and high operational costs. MMM leverages existing historical data without the need for expensive or complex experiments. However, it requires a longer period of historical data to accurately fit the model. 
ROI Agent Can we run the MMM with shorter period of time? 
No, we don’t suggest to do it, because too short and granular data may lead to model instability.  Weekly data with a historical lookback window of at least 2 months is recommended. This provides the best balance between strategic stability and tactical agility, giving the model enough data to produce reliable and actionable insights. 
ROI Agent How can you prove you really use Google MMM model? 
Our entire incrementality measurement framework is built directly upon Google's Meridian, which is an open-source project available for public review. Open-Source Verification: Your data science or analytics teams are welcome to review the model's source code and documentation on Google's official GitHub repository: github.com/google/meridian
