# 007 AIRIS - data warehouse concept paragraphs

Marketing-First Data Processing with AIRISIn today�s fast-paced marketing landscape, the ability to access and act on data in real time is critical. Traditional data warehouses, while essential for structured data storage, often fall short when it comes to delivering actionable insights quickly and efficiently. Marketing teams are increasingly burdened by fragmented data flows, manual ETL processes, and IT dependencies that slow down campaign execution and dilute the value of data over time.Marketers often rely on ETL and IT teams just to extract simple segments or generate reports. This not only introduces delays but also increases operational costs, making real-time engagement nearly impossible. High service fees for ETL and the overhead of internal IT communication make the entire process expensive and inefficient.Different marketing channels often require unique data formats, and most data uploads happen in batch rather than real time. This fragmented approach extends the processing timeline, reducing the immediacy and effectiveness of data. The longer it takes to act on the data, the more its value diminishes�resulting in missed opportunities and lower marketing ROI.AIRIS addresses these challenges by offering a seamless, marketing-first data solution that connects data storage, processing, and activation in one unified flow. It vertically integrates data from multiple sources, cleansing and standardizing it for consistency. Simultaneously, AIRIS enables horizontal delivery of real-time, cross-channel segmentation and activation�allowing marketing teams to engage the right users at the right time without relying on IT. This results in higher data efficiency, reduced operational cost, and increased revenue potential.
